How a partner goes live

The pipeline I built at CTCL

New election-data partners used to take weeks to get from a signed contract to live access. I redesigned the process end to end, working directly with partners and internal engineering. Here's what it looks like now.

Selection & Vetting

Assess partner fit, data needs, and technical readiness before a contract is signed.

Contract Signed

Kick off the internal handoff between legal, engineering, and partnerships.

Technical Onboarding

Configure access, credentials, and delivery pipelines alongside engineering.

Live Data Access

Partner is receiving live ballot and election data, tracked on a shared dashboard.

Renewal & Growth

Structured interviews and health metrics inform renewal and expansion conversations.

Visibility

Built and maintained partner-facing dashboards tracking data availability, delays, and completeness — giving every stakeholder the same real-time picture, and increasing partner satisfaction along the way.

Retention

Ran structured interviews with key partners each renewal cycle, turning feedback into concrete changes that improved satisfaction and retention across the base.

The problem

Before this process existed, a new partner could wait three to six weeks between a signed contract and their first live data pull. Handoffs between legal, engineering, and partnerships happened over scattered email threads with no single owner, so requests stalled while everyone assumed someone else was moving them forward.

What changed

I mapped every step a partner touched from signature to first delivery, then rebuilt it around one clear owner and a fixed checklist that engineering and partnerships could both see. Every new partner moved through the same five stages, with credential and pipeline setup starting the moment a contract was countersigned — not after.

How it was measured

I built partner-facing dashboards that tracked data availability, delays, and completeness in real time, so partners could see their own status instead of asking for updates. That same visibility let me catch internal bottlenecks before a partner had to flag them.

The result

Contract-to-live-access time dropped from multiple weeks to under 48 hours. Using that visibility and a structured interview process, I onboarded 6 new partner organizations and managed renewals for 4 existing partners, improving satisfaction and retention along the way.
